COGNIFYZ DATA ANALYSIS INTERNSHIP
LEVEL 3 - TASK 1: RESTAURANT REVIEWS

1. Objective
To analyse restaurant ratings and customer review information.

2. Dataset
The supplied dataset contains restaurant ratings and rating
categories, but does not contain written customer reviews.

3. Analysis Performed
- Counted restaurants in each rating category.
- Calculated average aggregate ratings by category.
- Created a bar chart showing rating category distribution.

4. Visualisation
File: task1_rating_categories.png

5. Limitation
The dataset does not contain written review text.
Therefore, positive and negative keyword analysis,
average review length, and review-length versus rating
analysis could not be performed.

6. Conclusion
The available rating categories provide an overview of
restaurant rating distribution. Further review analysis
requires a dataset containing actual customer review text.
